Blows weaken
In Ash & Crown pieces don't die. A struck piece becomes one step weaker:\nking → queen → rook → bishop → knight → pawn → ghost.\nThe attacking piece stays where it is.
Strike the black queen with the rook until it becomes a pawn
The rook on a1 strikes along the file — tap it, then the piece on a5
The queen went all the way down to a pawn, and the rook never left a1.
To get rid of a strong piece you have to strike it several times.
Ghost
A struck pawn becomes a ghost. A ghost moves one square in any direction but can't capture.\nStrike a ghost and it leaves the board, and the attacker takes its square.
Remove the black pawn from the board
Strike d5 with the rook — twice
The pawn became a ghost, and the ghost left the board.
Ghosts are harmless but block the way — sometimes you have to clear them too.
Revival
A piece weakened to a pawn remembers what it was. On reaching the last rank it revives as its original piece.\nThe pawn on e7 used to be a queen.
Move the pawn to e8
The pawn moves forward — to e8
The queen is back! A pawn that started as a pawn would become a queen.
A weakened piece can return to the fight if you get it to the end of the board.
Mate and fire
Kings are never captured — they get checked and mated. But mate doesn't end the game:\nthe king leaves the board, and 1–3 pieces of his army ignite and gain powers.
Checkmate the black king in one move
The king is boxed in by his own pawns. Think about the back rank
The black king has fallen, and his pieces ignited!
Now Black has no king and doesn't fear check. They win if they mate your king and lose when they run out of pieces.
Burning queen
Your king has fallen, but your pieces are burning. A burning queen also moves like a knight — in an “L”.
Strike the knight on f5 with a knight move
Select the queen on d4 — f5 is among its moves
An ordinary queen can't do that.
The burning queen is the most dangerous piece on the board.
Pierce
A burning rook or bishop can strike through one piece once per game — enemy or your own.\nSuch moves are highlighted in purple.
Strike the queen on a7 with the rook through the pawn
Select the rook on a1 — purple mark on a7
The pawn is intact, and the queen got weaker.
Strike the rook on g5 with the bishop through your own pawn
Select the bishop on c1 — purple mark on g5
You can go through your own pieces too.
Every burning rook and bishop has one pierce per game — save it.
A burning knight is a trojan knight. It carries one of your pieces, jumps twice once per game and can explode.
Board the bishop into the knight: select the bishop and tap the knight
The bishop on e3 stands next to the knight — the knight has a green ring
The bishop is inside. If the knight gets weakened, the passenger drops out nearby.
Make a double jump with the knight
Teal marks are the double jump. If it ends with a capture, the knight stays on the middle square
Two jumps in one move. This knight can't double jump again.
Explode the knight: select it and press the button to the right of the board
Select the knight — the “Explode knight” button appears
Boom! Every piece around got weaker — enemies and your own. The knight and its passenger are gone.
The explosion doesn't touch kings. Explode where many enemy pieces are close.
